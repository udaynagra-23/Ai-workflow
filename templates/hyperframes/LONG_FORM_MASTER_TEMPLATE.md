## Narrative architecture
1. **Cold open:** show a compelling question, contradiction, result, or moment from the story.
2. **Promise and orientation:** tell viewers what they will discover and establish essential context without a long channel introduction.
3. **Context:** explain the background needed to understand the central question.
4. **Chapter 1 — foundation:** establish the first key idea or event.
5. **Chapter 2 — development:** deepen the story with evidence, examples, and visual explanations.
6. **Chapter 3 — complication/reveal:** introduce the most important shift, uncertainty, obstacle, or surprising evidence.
7. **Chapter 4 — resolution/implications:** connect the pieces and answer the central question.
8. **Payoff:** explicitly deliver the promise made in the opening.
9. **Ending:** leave one memorable conclusion; use a CTA only if it feels natural.

## Narration and audio review
- Transcript matches approved script.
- Pronunciation and names checked.
- Delivery is natural and understandable.
- Pacing leaves room for complex visuals.
- Dialogue/narration is intelligible above music.
- Loudness and peaks reviewed where audio tools allow.
- Music/SFX rights and credits recorded.
- No audio repair is claimed unless processed and checked.

## Final editorial QA
- Opening delivers a compelling reason to watch.
- Video fulfils the stated promise.
- Facts, dates, names, and statistics checked against sources.
- Chapters progress logically and avoid repetition.
- Every visual adds information, atmosphere, or emotional meaning.
- Diagrams, maps, labels, and numbers are accurate.
- Captions match narration and remain readable.
- Motion is consistent and not distracting.
- Cuts, transitions, and audio joins are clean.
- No missing media, placeholders, black frames, or unintended gaps.
- Ending resolves the opening promise.
- Export settings match the approved brief.
