# Master Visual Identity — Proposal Pending Approval

This is a starting proposal for a polished, documentary/explainer-style channel identity. It is not an approved final brand system yet.

## Proposed direction
**Editorial cinematic clarity:** premium documentary atmosphere combined with clean, modern information graphics. The story and footage lead; animation supports understanding.

## Proposed design rules
- Canvas: 16:9 for long-form; adapt layouts to 9:16 only when producing Shorts.
- Palette: deep charcoal/near-black foundations, warm off-white text, and one restrained electric-cyan or amber accent. Select one accent after user approval.
- Typography: one bold, highly legible sans-serif for titles and one neutral sans-serif for body labels. Avoid decorative fonts.
- Composition: strong focal point, clear hierarchy, generous negative space, consistent safe margins.
- Captions: readable sentence-sized phrases, controlled line length, consistent placement and emphasis.
- Graphics: simple diagrams, timelines, maps, charts, and labels with consistent line weights and spacing.
- Motion: smooth fades, masked reveals, progressive builds, gentle camera moves, motivated cuts, and deliberate holds.
- Sound: clear narration first; subtle ambience and restrained sound accents; music should support rather than overpower.
- Transitions: primarily motivated cuts; a consistent short chapter transition for long-form.
- Branding: avoid a long animated logo intro. Use restrained branding where it does not delay the story.

## What must remain consistent
- Palette and typography.
- Caption treatment and safe margins.
- Diagram and data-card grammar.
- Motion intensity and transition language.
- Audio hierarchy and sound restraint.

## What can change per story
- Hero imagery, chapter motifs, accent use within the approved palette, diagram type, and scene pacing.

## Approval checkpoint
Before treating this as the channel's permanent identity, the user must approve or revise:
- Overall editorial/cinematic direction.
- Accent colour choice.
- Typography feel.
- Motion intensity.

Until approval, use this only as a proposal and do not describe it as the user's locked brand identity.
