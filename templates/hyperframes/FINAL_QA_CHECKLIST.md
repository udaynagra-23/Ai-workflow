# Final Video QA — Independent Recheck

Perform this pass separately from drafting/composition. Review the video or preview itself when available, not just the script.

## A. Story and research
- [ ] Title and opening accurately represent the video.
- [ ] Opening earns attention and sets a clear promise.
- [ ] Narrative has a logical beginning, development, and payoff.
- [ ] All key claims, dates, names, and figures have reliable source notes.
- [ ] Uncertainty and differing interpretations are represented fairly.
- [ ] No unsupported claim or misleading visual implication.
- [ ] Repetition and filler removed.
- [ ] All opening questions/open loops are resolved.

## B. Script and narration
- [ ] Spoken language sounds natural.
- [ ] Names and technical terms have pronunciation notes where needed.
- [ ] Sentences are clear when heard once.
- [ ] Pacing allows viewers to process complex information.
- [ ] Narration matches the approved script or deviations are logged.
- [ ] Audio levels and clarity checked where an actual audio preview/tool is available.

## C. Visual storytelling
- [ ] Every scene has a clear purpose.
- [ ] Visuals support the narration rather than merely decorate it.
- [ ] Reused templates match the scene and remain consistent.
- [ ] Animation is smooth, purposeful, and readable.
- [ ] Diagrams, maps, charts, and labels are accurate.
- [ ] Framing and safe margins work on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Captions are accurate, synchronized, and legible.
- [ ] No unlicensed, missing, placeholder, stretched, or low-quality assets.
- [ ] No accidental blank frames, flash frames, or abrupt unintended cuts.

## D. Audio and finishing
- [ ] Narration remains intelligible above music and effects.
- [ ] Music and sound effects fit the mood and have rights/source recorded.
- [ ] No unexpected clipping, silence, pops, or abrupt audio joins.
- [ ] Ending has enough space to land.
- [ ] Export aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, and audio format match the brief.

## E. Recheck protocol
For every issue:
1. Assign severity: blocker / major / minor.
2. Record scene/timecode and exact issue.
3. Apply a fix without changing approved creative direction.
4. Re-open/review the affected scene.
5. Mark rechecked only after verifying the correction.

## F. Release gate
- [ ] No unresolved blockers or major issues.
- [ ] Remaining minor issues disclosed.
- [ ] User has reviewed and approved the final version.
- [ ] Any credit-consuming render or publishing action has explicit approval.

**Preview unavailable:** mark visual/audio checks as not verified. Never mark the video as passed based only on a written plan.
